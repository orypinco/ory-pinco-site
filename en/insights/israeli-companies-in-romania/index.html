Doing Business in Romania: What Israeli Companies Should Know

For Israeli companies looking at European expansion, Romania can offer an interesting combination of market access, talent and room for growth.

But entering Romania is about more than registering a company. Companies need to understand how the local business environment works and how to turn a market opportunity into a functioning operation.

The first questions are practical. Should the company begin with an EOR solution or establish a Romanian entity? Where should the first employees be hired? Which local partners and suppliers are needed? And who will manage the operation on the ground?

Business culture also matters. International companies may be familiar with the formal requirements of entering a new market, but local relationships, communication and day to day execution can make a significant difference.

For Israeli companies in particular, Romania can also be considered as a European base while maintaining close coordination with the Israeli headquarters through our Romania ↔ Israel desk.

A successful market entry therefore requires more than legal setup. It requires a clear plan for people, operations, finance, suppliers and local management.

The objective is simple: not just to enter Romania, but to build an operation that can actually work and grow.
